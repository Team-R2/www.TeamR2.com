Text updates

Get a text when your order is ready.

Sign up for R2 Fabrication Order & Delivery Updates and we’ll text you when your order is received, ready for pickup, scheduled for delivery, and delivered.

Your info

What you’ll get

Texts about your order only: received, ready for pickup, delivery scheduled, out for delivery, delivered, and schedule changes, plus one-to-one texts with the R2 staff member handling your order. No advertising.

How often

Message frequency varies: order updates are typically 3–5 messages per order, plus any back-and-forth with R2 staff. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message. Reply HELP, call 817.720.6060, or email info@TeamR2.com for help.

Rather sign up in person?

Tell us when you place your order at the shop or by phone. Our consent form has the same terms.
